## About

I build **production-grade systems and research tools** at the intersection of **software engineering, AI, and geospatial science**. My work spans from **cross-platform mobile & web applications** to **data-driven research infrastructure**.

I believe in engineering that balances:
- **Architectural rigor** – clean, maintainable, testable code
- **Real-world impact** – tools that solve genuine problems
- **Evidence-driven results** – research backed by reproducible methodology

Whether I'm shipping a multi-platform Flutter app with 250k+ message processing, building AI-powered documentation generators with parallel agents, or computing rural healthcare accessibility across 5,000+ villages, I focus on systems that are technically strong, well-documented, and grounded in measurable outcomes.
